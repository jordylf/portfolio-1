Welcome!

Hi! I’m Jordy Lian Ferdinand, also known as Ody.
I was born in Jakarta, Indonesia, on February 12, and I’m a recent university graduate. I’m a graphic designer, motion designer, and backend developer with over 7 years of experience in motion design and video editing. I started learning and using Adobe After Effects in 2017, and since then I’ve been passionate about creating motion graphics, compositing, and visual content using tools like Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator. I began by creating AMVs and sharing them on Instagram, which helped me develop my style and skills. Today, I focus on producing music videos, intros, and streaming visuals—while also building backend systems to support digital projects. I enjoy blending creativity and technology to create work that feels clean, engaging, and purposeful.
